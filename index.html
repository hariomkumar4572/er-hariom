Full Architectural Overview

Mushibat Healthcare

Digital Healthcare Appointment & Queue Elimination System
The Core Problem

In traditional hospital setups, patients endure lengthy physical queues, unclear doctor availability schedules, and emergency delays. Mushibat Healthcare was engineered to eliminate unnecessary waiting times by digitizing the complete intake lifecycle.

End-to-End Workflow
  • Hospital Search: Patients discover accredited hospitals with real-time department status.
  • Doctor Selection: Transparent physician profiles, specialties, and consult slots.
  • Appointment Booking: Real-time reservation with instant confirmation.
  • Patient Details: Streamlined intake form capturing vitals and prior history.
  • Emergency vs. Normal: Priority triage queuing to route urgent cases instantly.
  • Digital Token Info: Digital appointment slip with QR verification for desk check-ins.
Technologies Used
JavaScript HTML5 & CSS3 Vite Build Engine Responsive UI Architecture Local Storage / State Management
